\hypertarget{index_autotoc_md13}{}\doxysection{\texorpdfstring{Gaussian Quadrature}{Gaussian Quadrature}}\label{index_autotoc_md13}
Based on Hagedorn’s Master’s Thesis (Chapter 4.\+4.\+3), we choose 8 optimal energy/weight pairs for integrating over the dose spectrum. ~\newline

\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item {\bfseries{Quadrature energies}}\+: e.\+g. {\ttfamily \mbox{[}11.\+5, 20.\+0, …\mbox{]}} keV ~\newline

\item {\bfseries{Weights}}\+: corresponding integration weights ~\newline

\end{DoxyItemize}

The GUI lets you pick an {\bfseries{energy range}}; we then compute the 8 quadrature points \& weights on the fly and feed them into {\ttfamily Inference\+Module}.
